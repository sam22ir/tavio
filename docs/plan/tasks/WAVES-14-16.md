# Waves 14–16: correct numbers, Algerian legal, links between apps

Source: [APP_AUDIT.md](../APP_AUDIT.md) (2026-10-10). Three waves, in this order:
- **Wave 14** fixes money that is wrong today.
- **Wave 15** makes the documents and the accounting legal in Algeria.
- **Wave 16** links the apps together the way Odoo does.

Inside a wave, tasks run in parallel (Haiku agents, one worktree each). Wave 13 is the Mourabaha CRM / credit workflow / deliveries work merged on 2026-10-10; it already covers part of 14-C (contract → reservations and delivery orders) and each task owns its files. Every wave ends with:
- integration
- one migration, generated by the integrator
- tests
- local screenshots for review
- deploy, only after approval

Sizes: S ≈ half a day of agent work, M ≈ 1 day, L ≈ 2+ days.

## Wave 14: correct numbers (fix first)

**Status (2026-10-10): done.** 14-A, 14-B, 14-C and 14-D merged, migrations 0013–0015 applied. Vendor bills now post stock-tracked goods to 380 so that receipts (Dr 30 / Cr 380) don't double-count. Partial delivery (delivered qty per line) moved to 16-C. 14-E (data check) skipped: test data only.

| Task | What | Size |
|---|---|---|
| **14-A Line discounts end to end** | Add `discount_bp` (basis points) to invoice lines. `computeLine` applies it before tax. `createInvoiceFromOrder` passes the sale line discount through. Invoice form, list totals and print show the discount column. Unit tests cover rounding at 0, 5% and 12.5% on DZD cents. | S |
| **14-B Purchase billing by billed quantity** | Add `billed_qty` per PO line, replacing the single `billed` flag. A bill takes received − billed. `canBill` = any line with received > billed. State `billed` when everything received is billed. The PO page lists the bills linked to it. Tests: receive 5 → bill → receive 5 more → bill again. | S |
| **14-C Credit contract moves stock** | On `credit_sales.contract.activated`, inventory creates and posts delivery moves (internal → customer) for the contract lines. Cancelling an active contract creates the return moves. The contract page shows "Livré" (delivered) with a link to the moves. Stock goods only; services are skipped. | S |
| **14-D Perpetual stock valuation (after D1)** | `unit_cost` on stock moves. Receipts at PO price, deliveries at current CMUP. Product `avg_cost` is updated on each receipt. Moves post entries: receipt Dr 30 / Cr 38; delivery Dr 600 / Cr 30 (the SCF mapping is a setting, flagged for the accountant). Adds the class-3 accounts to the seed. Adds a stock valuation report per warehouse. Runs after 14-C, because both touch inventory events. | M |
| **14-E Data check** | A script reports existing invoices that lost discounts and POs with unbilled receipts. Test data only today, so it just reports and doesn't rewrite. | S |

Order: 14-A, 14-B and 14-C run in parallel, then 14-D, then 14-E. **Output:** money, stock and accounting agree.

## Wave 15: Algerian legal and printing

**Status (2026-10-10): done and deployed.** 15-0, 15-A, 15-B, 15-C, 15-D, 15-E plus withholding tax on vendor bills, an editable company identity form, translated SCF statements, and demo stock + accounting repairs. Migrations 0016–0017. Everything tax, account or layout related is flagged for the accountant; the contract clauses for a lawyer. Known gap: freshly seeded sample data has no delivery/sales stock moves (only the repair adds them).

| Task | What | Size |
|---|---|---|
| **15-0 Import/export engine** (shared, first) | One small library for CSV + XLSX that is light enough for Workers CPU (checked with a CPU test). Export: any DataList gets "Exporter" (Export) with the current filters, up to 10k rows, with a background job above that. Import: upload → column mapping → preview with errors → confirm, for contacts, products and leads first. Every page in Wave 15 and Wave 16 reuses it. | M |
| **15-A Shared document layout** | One print layout used by quote, sale order, invoice, credit note, purchase order, payment receipt and POS ticket. It carries the company identity (name, NIF, NIS, RC, AI, address, logo, RIB/CCP), the customer identity (with NIF for B2B), VAT by rate, the stamp duty line, the amount in words (fr/ar) and paid / balance due. POS ticket: 80mm version with the same identity. Adds the payment receipt print. | M |
| **15-B Credit contract clauses + guarantor** | Contract clauses become an editable template per company in settings, in fr and ar, with a default text written for lawyer review (D6) and the variables filled in (parties, schedule, penalties). Guarantor: a form on the contract (existing table), shown on the detail page and the print, with a signature block for the guarantor. | M |
| **15-C Taxes and G50** | Company tax regime setting (réel / IFU). Tax types beyond TVA: TAP (if it still applies to the regime; the accountant confirms the rate), and withholding (retenue à la source) on supplier services. **G50:** a saved declaration per month, with draft → validated (locks the period's VAT), print in the official layout, and export. **Stamp duty:** tiers become a setting. IBS/IRG: the annual figures are computed from the TCR (15-D). IRG on salaries waits for payroll. | M |
| **15-D Accounting core** | **Chart of accounts:** the full SCF chart (classes 1–7) as an editable seed (add, edit, archive), custom journals, and draft → posted for manual entries. **Closing:** fiscal years and period lock (no posting before the lock date), a closing entry plus carry-forward of opening balances. **Reports:** bilan (actif / passif) and TCR in the SCF layout, plus grand livre and balance with opening balances. Everything exports to Excel/PDF via 15-0, and there's an "Export comptable" ZIP for the accountant (journals, ledger, balance). | L |
| **15-E Bank and cash reconciliation** | Import a bank statement (CSV via 15-0), then match lines with payments and entries: auto-match by amount + reference + date, then manual matching. Reconciled marks on 512 and 53. A cash count for the 53 account. | M |

Order: 15-0, then 15-A, 15-B and 15-C in parallel, with 15-D starting at the same time, then 15-E. **Output:** every printed document is legal, the accountant can work from exports, and closing works.

## Wave 16: links between apps (Odoo parity)

| Task | What | Size |
|---|---|---|
| **16-A Related records ("smart buttons")** | A generic `RelatedLinks` bar on detail pages showing counts and links. Contact: leads, quotes/orders, credit contracts, invoices, payments, tickets, POS orders. Product: stock per warehouse, moves, sales, purchases. SO: deliveries, invoices. Invoice: payments, origin order. Contract: moves, entries. It's driven by a registry, so each module declares its relations once. | M |
| **16-B CRM ↔ sales ↔ website** | Lead → "Nouveau devis" (new quote) creates a quote linked to the lead; the credit contract option stays. A won quote closes the lead. Website contact form → lead (source "site web"). A setting makes a web order become a sale order automatically. The web order shows its SO, invoice and payment status. | M |
| **16-C Deliveries and invoicing by delivered quantity** | A delivery document (bon de livraison) groups an SO's moves: validate fully or partially, with backorders and a printed BL (delivery note). Invoice policy per product (ordered / delivered). SO lines track delivered and invoiced qty. Several invoices per order. Cancelling an SO cancels its open deliveries. Depends on 14-A. | L |
| **16-D Import/export everywhere** | Export on every list. Import added for sales orders, stock (opening quantities), the chart of accounts and opening balances, using the 15-0 engine. | S |
| **16-E Customer portal** | Customers sign in by magic link (email or WhatsApp) per company. They see their invoices, credit contracts with the schedule and what's paid or due, receipts, and PDF downloads. Online payment via Chargily (D4), recorded automatically as a payment. Share link from the invoice and the contract. | L |
| **16-F Real messaging** | One `messaging` service with channels: email (Resend, already set up), WhatsApp Cloud API (D2), and SMS (D3, optional). Templates per company in fr/ar. An outbox with retries and delivery status. Used by credit reminders (automatic, by schedule), marketing campaigns (bulk), "Envoyer" (send) on quote, invoice and contract, and helpdesk replies. Opt-out list. | L |

Order: 16-A, 16-B and 16-D in parallel, then 16-C, then 16-E and 16-F in parallel (they're independent). **Output:** the flow works end to end, from lead → quote → order → delivery → invoice → payment → accounting, with the customer seeing and paying online and reminders sent automatically.
